# Frieren: Beyond Journey's End (*Sousou no Frieren*)

> *"It's just a mere ten years... Why didn't I try to get to know him better?"* — **Frieren**

---

## Synopsis

**Frieren: Beyond Journey's End** takes place *after* the Hero's Party has already defeated the Demon King and brought peace to the world. As an elven mage with a nearly infinite lifespan, Frieren watches her former comrades slowly pass away of old age. 

Realizing how little she understood the people who meant the most to her, she embarks on a new journey toward **Aureole** (where souls rest) alongside a new generation of companions—retracing the exact footsteps of her 10-year adventure with **Himmel the Hero**.

## Why It's My Favorite Series

1. **Unique Premise:** It starts where most fantasy stories end—after the final boss is already defeated.
2. **Masterful World-Building:** Every town carries the quiet legacy of the Hero's Party from decades ago.
3. **Incredible Animation & Score:** Produced by Studio Madhouse with an emotional soundtrack composed by Evan Call.
4. **Themes of Time and Memory:** It highlights how even small, everyday moments with friends become priceless memories.
